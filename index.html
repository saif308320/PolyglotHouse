The House

Founded 2018 on Academic Row for working adults who were tired of apps that never speak back.

Teaching philosophy

We do not treat languages as museums. Students speak from day one. Tutors are coaches. Every hour has an output.

Placement is honest. If you are A2, we will not sell you C1 because a quiz said so. Corporate groups get the same standard as evening adults.

Do I need to live in Dublin?

No. Live rooms are hybrid. Self-paced is fully remote. 1:1 can be online.

Which CEFR levels do you cover?

A1 to C1 for Spanish and French. A1 to B2 for Japanese and Arabic, with C1 by arrangement.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Purchase orders and monthly invoices. Training reports on request.

What if I miss a live class?

Recording stays up 72 hours. You can jump a makeup seat once per month.
